**Puzzle Mechanics Discovered:**
* The command "move rug" in Living Room (R193) reveals a hidden trap door beneath the oriental rug. "lift rug", "push rug", "examine rug" do NOT work — only "move rug".
* Opening the trap door after moving the rug provides access to the Cellar (R25) below.
* The troll in Troll Room (R72) can be defeated by attacking with the elvish sword. Multiple attacks required.
* Pressing the red button in the Maintenance Room (R?) activates the lights and raises the water level to the knees.
* Pressing the blue, yellow, or brown buttons in the Maintenance Room (R?) causes water to leak or click, but does not affect the water level.
* Dropping the leaflet in the Maintenance Room (R?) does not trigger any special effect.
